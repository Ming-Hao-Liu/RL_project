# Reinforcement Learning Final Project / 强化学习期末项目

## RL Methods and Research Applications / 强化学习方法与研究应用

The final project accounts for 50% of the course grade and will be completed in teams of no more than 5 students. Teams will apply, adapt, or theoretically analyze reinforcement learning methods to address a research question, ideally connected to their own interests.

期末项目占课程成绩的 50%，以不超过 5 人的小组完成。各组将应用、改造或从理论上分析强化学习方法，以解决一个研究问题；该问题最好与成员自身的兴趣相关。

## Example directions / 示例方向

- **Engineering — Humanoid robot control.** Build on OpenHomie to investigate reward design, training curricula, or sim-to-real transfer.  
  Paper: [HOMIE: Humanoid Loco-Manipulation with Isomorphic Exoskeleton Cockpit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13013) | [Official GitHub code](https://github.com/InternRobotics/OpenHomie)

  **工程——人形机器人控制。** 基于 OpenHomie 研究奖励设计、训练课程或从仿真到现实的迁移。  
  论文：[HOMIE: Humanoid Loco-Manipulation with Isomorphic Exoskeleton Cockpit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13013) | [官方 GitHub 代码](https://github.com/InternRobotics/OpenHomie)

- **Algorithms — Preference optimization.** Investigate how preference-data quality or regularization affects language-model alignment using DPO.  
  Paper: [Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Rewar...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) | [Official GitHub code](https://github.com/eric-mitchell/direct-preference-optimization)

  **算法——偏好优化。** 使用 DPO 研究偏好数据质量或正则化如何影响语言模型对齐。  
  论文：[Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Rewar...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) | [官方 GitHub 代码](https://github.com/eric-mitchell/direct-preference-optimization)

- **Theory — Performance guarantees.** Analyze an RL algorithm and establish an error, convergence, or regret bound under clearly stated assumptions.  
  Paper: [Is Q-learning Provably Efficient?](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03765)

  **理论——性能保证。** 分析一种强化学习算法，并在明确陈述的假设下建立误差界、收敛界或遗憾界。  
  论文：[Is Q-learning Provably Efficient?](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03765)

Publication-level contributions are not required. A few well-motivated modifications to existing open-source code, supported by clear experiments and analysis, are sufficient. Theoretical projects may investigate a focused extension or a simplified setting of an existing result.

不要求达到发表论文的贡献水平。对现有开源代码做少量动机充分的修改，并辅以清晰的实验和分析即可。理论项目可以研究对已有结果的一个聚焦扩展，或其简化情形。

Limited course-owned robot arms and humanoid robots may be available for course use. Students may also use their own hardware and should arrange their own computing resources.

课程可能提供数量有限的机械臂和人形机器人供课程使用。学生也可以使用自己的硬件，并应自行安排计算资源。

## Marking standard / 评分标准

| Criterion / 评分项 | Weight / 权重 |
| --- | --- |
| Clear problem formulation and motivation / 问题表述与研究动机清晰 | 20% |
| Technical correctness and depth / 技术正确性与深度 | 20% |
| Rigorous evaluation or theoretical analysis / 严谨的评估或理论分析 | 20% |
| Quality of the report, presentation, and supporting materials / 报告、展示及支撑材料的质量 | 20% |
| Individual contribution and understanding / 个人贡献与理解 | 20% |

These weights apply to the project grade. All directions will be assessed fairly, emphasizing understanding, careful investigation, and clearly explained contributions. High marks do not require expensive hardware, large computing budgets, or positive results; well-analyzed negative findings are also valuable.

上述权重适用于项目成绩。所有方向都将得到公平评价，重点关注理解程度、细致的研究和清晰阐述的贡献。取得高分不要求昂贵的硬件、大量计算资源或正面结果；经过充分分析的负面发现同样有价值。
